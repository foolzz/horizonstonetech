Professional Services — Regional accounting firm

Document intake & classification.

Clients send receipts and statements in every format imaginable. We turned a shared inbox into a pipeline that extracts, categorizes, and posts to the firm's bookkeeping platform — with a human approval step.

12hrs
saved per week
The problem

Staff were manually opening, reading, and re-keying client documents from a shared inbox into the bookkeeping system.

What we built

Clients keep dropping documents into the same inbox. The system extracts the key fields, classifies each document, and prepares the entry; a staff member approves it before it posts.

Outcome

12 hours saved per week, with a person still signing off on every entry.
