Voice-AI appointment confirmation.

The problem

Confirmation calls were manual, repetitive, and ate into the front desk's afternoon — and missed appointments still slipped through.

What we built

A voice agent that calls patients ahead of their appointment in English, Mandarin, or Cantonese, confirms or flags a reschedule, and records the result for the front desk.

Outcome

84% fewer no-shows, measured after launch. The front desk got their afternoons back.
