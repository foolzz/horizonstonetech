Why we built it

Newcomers face the same question again and again: where can we afford to live, and where will our income go furthest? The answers are scattered across government sites, in English only.

What it does
  • Enter income and household details; the calculator ranks provinces by estimated monthly surplus after tax and expenses.
  • Available in English, Simplified Chinese, Hindi, Punjabi, and Tagalog.
  • Guides on healthcare, cities, and moving from the US — the largest share of visitors.
How it's built

Deliberately boring: plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with no framework and no backend. It's fast, costs nothing to run on Cloudflare Pages, and grows through search.

Stack
  • HTML + CSS + vanilla JS
  • Five-language i18n
  • Cloudflare Pages
  • SEO-first content
